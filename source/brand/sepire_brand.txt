Sepire Brand

Sepire uses a warm, professional, and modern brand style focused on secure customer communications, accessibility, and compliance.

The tone is friendly, reliable, and trustworthy, supported by clean layouts, strong whitespace, and visible compliance cues such as HITRUST, SOC2, and PCI.

Core colors:
Primary Orange: #E5833B
Charcoal Gray: #333333
White: #FFFFFF
Peach/Sand Accent: #FDECE0

Typography should use clean geometric sans-serif fonts such as Montserrat, Poppins, Inter, or Helvetica Neue.

Design elements include pill-shaped orange CTA buttons, light halftone dot patterns, minimalist navigation, generous whitespace, and use of the Sepire “S” mark as an icon.

For execution, use darker orange hover states, place dot patterns around edges or corners, and keep the main headline as the strongest visual element.
